About Kay

Therapy rooted in warmth, real life, and lasting change

You're the one everyone else depends on. You show up for your family, your job, your friends, and somehow keep all the plates spinning. From the outside, it looks like you're handling everything. But inside? You're exhausted. You're constantly overthinking, overwhelmed by the mental load, and wondering why everything feels so much harder than it seems to for everyone else.

Maybe you've recently become a mom and don't quite recognize yourself anymore. Maybe you've spent years wondering why staying organized, managing emotions, or keeping up with daily life feels like such a struggle, only to discover ADHD later in life. Or maybe you're simply at a crossroads — burned out at work, disconnected in your relationship, or questioning what's next.

My approach is warm, down-to-earth, and practical. Therapy with me isn't about pretending everything is okay or endlessly rehashing the past. It's about understanding why you're feeling stuck, learning skills that actually fit your real life, and creating meaningful changes that help you feel more like yourself again.

Together, we'll work to quiet the constant pressure to be perfect, reduce overwhelm, build healthier boundaries, and create a life that feels sustainable — not just productive. You deserve more than just getting through the day. You deserve to enjoy your life again.

Common Questions

What insurances do you accept?+

I accept most major insurances including BCBS, Medicare, Nebraska Medicaid, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, CHAMPVA, Medica, Lyra Health, and Midlands Choice. Self-pay is also accepted. Contact me to verify your specific coverage.

Do you offer in-person and telehealth?+

Yes — I offer both! In-person sessions are available at my Blair, NE office (403 S 16th St Suite B). Telehealth sessions are available to clients anywhere in Nebraska via secure video.

Do you specialize in postpartum or perinatal mental health?+

Yes — I hold the PMH-C (Perinatal Mental Health Certified) credential and specialize in supporting women through pregnancy, postpartum, and the identity shifts of new motherhood. You don't have to navigate this season alone.

Do you work with women with ADHD?+

Absolutely — ADHD in women is one of my specialty areas. Whether you were recently diagnosed or have known for years, therapy can help you understand your brain, build systems that work for your real life, and reduce the shame and overwhelm that often comes with ADHD.

What happens in the first session?+

We start with a free 15-minute phone consultation to make sure we're a good fit. Your first full session is a chance to share what brought you to therapy and what you're hoping to work on. There's no pressure — we go at your pace.

How do I get started?+

Click "Book My Appointment" to request a free consultation through the secure client portal, or send a message using the Contact button above. Kay responds within 24 hours.
