Meet the Team

Meet Kay Arispe, LICSW, PMH-C

I’m a Licensed Independent Clinical Social Worker and Perinatal Mental Health - Certified therapist who believes therapy can be both meaningful and practical. My style is warm, down-to-earth, collaborative, and grounded in real life. I want clients to feel comfortable being themselves in the room while also leaving with greater understanding, useful tools, and a clearer sense of how to move forward.

My clinical work is especially informed by an interest in women’s mental health, ADHD, pregnancy and postpartum concerns, anxiety, depression, trauma, burnout, relationships, and the many transitions that can change how we see ourselves and our lives. I tend to work best with people who are carrying a lot, thinking about a lot, and ready for a space where they do not have to have everything figured out before they walk in.

I’m not a one-size-fits-all therapist. I draw from evidence-based approaches and tailor therapy to the person in front of me, balancing insight with practical strategies that can actually be used outside of session. I value honesty, humor when it fits, curiosity, and helping people understand the patterns underneath what they are experiencing rather than simply telling them what they “should” do.

Sage Therapy & Consulting grew out of that same practical, individualized philosophy. I built my private practice from the ground up, learning the business side along the way, and that experience eventually became the foundation for the consulting work I now offer to other clinicians. As Sage grows into a group practice, my goal is to build a team that shares the same commitment to thoughtful, approachable, high-quality care.

For Clinicians

Practice-building support from someone who has been there

When I decided to start my own private practice, I built it on my own. I was self-taught, and I was scared — but I was also determined to make it happen. As I figured out each step along the way, I realized how little the business and practice-building side of clinical social work is taught in school. We learn how to be clinicians, but there isn't a manual for how to take those skills and build a private practice around them.

That experience shapes the way I work with clinicians today. My consulting services are individualized because every clinician, practice, and starting point is different. I don't offer a blanket course or a one-size-fits-all “how to” program. Instead, I sit down with you individually, learn where you are in the process, and focus on exactly what you need help with or where you're getting stuck as you work toward launching your practice.

I use my own real-world experience of building a practice from the ground up to relate to the uncertainty, questions, and decisions that come with starting one. My goal is to help you sort through what's in front of you, provide practical guidance, and make the process feel more manageable as you build a practice that fits you.

Therapy Modalities

Kay integrates different therapeutic approaches rather than using a one-size-fits-all model. Depending on each client’s needs, therapy with Kay may include:

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT) Interpersonal Psychotherapy (IPT) DBT-Informed Therapy Solution-Focused Therapy Internal Family Systems-Informed Approach
Coming in 2027: Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) is a structured therapy approach designed to help people process distressing or traumatic experiences and reduce the emotional impact those experiences can continue to have in the present. Kay plans to begin EMDR training in October 2026, with the goal of offering EMDR to clients after the first of the year once appropriate training requirements have been completed.

Private Practice Consulting

Build a Practice That Actually Works for You

Starting or growing a private practice can feel like a strange combination of exciting, overwhelming, and why did no one tell me about this part?

At Sage Therapy and Consulting, we offer practical, one-on-one consulting for therapists who want support navigating the business side of private practice. Whether you are starting from scratch, trying to get credentialed with insurance, setting up your systems, or realizing your current processes need some serious attention, consulting is designed to help you move forward with more clarity and less trial and error.

This is practical support from someone who understands both clinical work and the realities of running a therapy practice.

Consulting Services

Private Practice Consulting

$150 | 60 minutes

A focused one-on-one consultation for clinicians who are in the contemplative stage of launching a private practice, rebranding an existing practice, or considering expansion.

This session is less about working through every operational detail and more about helping you think through the bigger-picture questions: Is now the right time? What might need to happen first? What decisions still need clarity? What barriers or practical considerations should you think through before moving forward?

Together, we will identify what deserves your attention now and what your most useful next steps may be.

Practice Strategy Intensive

$225 | 90 minutes

A longer strategy session for clinicians who are ready to work through several specific practice questions or operational areas in more depth.

Depending on complexity, a 90-minute intensive can typically focus on approximately 3–4 topics such as:

  • Insurance credentialing and payer enrollment
  • CAQH and NPI setup
  • EHR and SimplePractice workflows
  • Billing and claims processes
  • Intake and documentation systems
  • Practice policies and procedures
  • Fee-setting and financial considerations
  • Adding clinicians or independent contractors
  • Practice organization and efficiency

You will leave with clearer priorities and actionable next steps for the areas we address.

Practice Launch Package

$695

For therapists who want more comprehensive support while starting their private practice.

The Practice Launch Package includes:

  • One 90-minute initial planning session
  • Two 60-minute follow-up consulting sessions
  • One 30-minute follow-up session
  • Private practice startup checklist
  • Guidance with EHR setup and workflows
  • Insurance credentialing roadmap
  • CAQH and NPI guidance
  • Billing and claims workflow planning
  • Intake and documentation workflow planning
  • Fee and payment considerations
  • Policies and forms checklist
  • Personalized next-step recommendations throughout the process

Rather than trying to figure everything out at once, we will break the process into manageable steps and focus on what actually needs your attention.

Common Questions

What insurances do you accept?+

For therapy services, I accept BCBS, Medicare, Nebraska Medicaid, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, CHAMPVA, Medica, Lyra Health, and Midlands Choice, and self-pay is also available. Insurance applies to eligible therapy services only and cannot be used for consulting. Contact me to verify therapy coverage or ask about consulting fees.

Do you offer in-person and telehealth?+

Yes — I offer both! In-person sessions are available at my Blair, NE office (403 S 16th St Suite B). Telehealth sessions are available to clients across Nebraska and Iowa via secure video.

What if I don’t have insurance?+

If you don’t have health insurance coverage for therapy, it may be worth checking with your employer to see whether you have access to Lyra Health or another employee assistance benefit. I accept Lyra EAP when available. I also offer a prompt-pay discount for clients who are uninsured and paying for therapy themselves. Please contact me for current fee and payment information.

Do you provide couples or family therapy?+

No. Sage Therapy & Consulting currently provides individual therapy and does not offer couples or family therapy.

Do you work with children or teens?+

I do not provide therapy for children. Services for teens ages 13 and older will be coming soon. Please check back for updates about availability for adolescent clients.

What happens in the first session?+

New therapy clients first complete the secure contact form. We review your needs and availability to determine whether the practice is an appropriate fit and which therapist may best match what you’re looking for before offering scheduling. Once you begin, your first full session is a chance to share what brought you to therapy and what you’re hoping to work on.

How do I get started?+

For therapy, complete the secure contact form first so we can review your needs, availability, and fit before scheduling. Clinicians interested in consulting can book a free 15-minute discovery call through the client portal.
