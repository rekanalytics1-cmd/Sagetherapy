Where growth and healing meet.

Sage Therapy & Consulting offers two distinct kinds of support. Mental health therapy provides a confidential space for clients to work through emotional challenges, strengthen coping skills, understand patterns, and create meaningful change in their lives. Consulting services are designed specifically for clinicians and focus on the practical side of building and growing a private practice, including clarifying goals, developing systems, and navigating practice ownership. Consulting is a professional service and is separate from mental health treatment.

Sage Therapy & Consulting

Support for your well-being—and your next chapter

We provide therapy for women and men experiencing depression, anxiety, trauma, ADHD, stress, burnout, and the pressures that can leave them feeling overwhelmed or disconnected from themselves. We also specialize in perinatal mental health, offering support through pregnancy, postpartum, and the many emotional challenges that can accompany the transition to parenthood.

In a welcoming, collaborative environment, we help clients gain clarity, develop practical tools, and create meaningful, lasting change. Sage Therapy and Consulting is located in Blair, Nebraska, and we also provide convenient online therapy for clients throughout Nebraska and Iowa.

In addition to clinical services, we offer consulting for therapists who are ready to launch and grow their own private practices. From clarifying your vision and establishing essential systems to navigating the realities of practice ownership, we provide practical guidance to help you move forward with confidence and direction.

Whether you’re seeking support for yourself or taking the next step toward practice ownership, Sage Therapy and Consulting is here to help you move forward.

About Kay

Therapy rooted in warmth, real life, and lasting change

You're the one everyone else depends on. You show up for your family, your job, your friends, and somehow keep all the plates spinning. From the outside, it looks like you're handling everything. But inside? You're exhausted. You're constantly overthinking, overwhelmed by the mental load, and wondering why everything feels so much harder than it seems to for everyone else.

Maybe you've recently become a mom and don't quite recognize yourself anymore. Maybe you've spent years wondering why staying organized, managing emotions, or keeping up with daily life feels like such a struggle, only to discover ADHD later in life. Or maybe you're simply at a crossroads — burned out at work, disconnected in your relationship, or questioning what's next.

My approach is warm, down-to-earth, and practical. Therapy with me isn't about pretending everything is okay or endlessly rehashing the past. It's about understanding why you're feeling stuck, learning skills that actually fit your real life, and creating meaningful changes that help you feel more like yourself again.

Together, we'll work to quiet the constant pressure to be perfect, reduce overwhelm, build healthier boundaries, and create a life that feels sustainable — not just productive. You deserve more than just getting through the day. You deserve to enjoy your life again.

Training & Clinical Approach

Evidence-based care, tailored to you

Therapy is collaborative and individualized. I draw from several evidence-based approaches based on your goals, needs, and what feels most useful in your life.

Perinatal Mental Health - Certified

I hold the PMH-C credential through Postpartum Support International (PSI). This certification reflects specialized training in perinatal mental health and supports my work with clients during pregnancy, postpartum, and the transition to parenthood.

Therapy Modalities

I integrate different therapeutic approaches rather than using a one-size-fits-all model. Depending on your needs, our work may include:

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT) Interpersonal Psychotherapy (IPT) DBT-Informed Therapy Solution-Focused Therapy Internal Family Systems-Informed Approach
Coming in 2027: Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) is a structured therapy approach designed to help people process distressing or traumatic experiences and reduce the emotional impact those experiences can continue to have in the present. I plan to begin EMDR training in October 2026, with the goal of offering EMDR to clients after the first of the year once appropriate training requirements have been completed.

Insurance & Payment

Insurance is accepted for eligible therapy services only. Insurance cannot be used to pay for consulting services. Consulting is a separate, self-pay professional service. Contact Kay with questions about therapy coverage or consulting fees.

Common Questions

What insurances do you accept?+

For therapy services, I accept BCBS, Medicare, Nebraska Medicaid, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, CHAMPVA, Medica, Lyra Health, and Midlands Choice, and self-pay is also available. Insurance applies to eligible therapy services only and cannot be used for consulting. Contact me to verify therapy coverage or ask about consulting fees.

Do you offer in-person and telehealth?+

Yes — I offer both! In-person sessions are available at my Blair, NE office (403 S 16th St Suite B). Telehealth sessions are available to clients across Nebraska and Iowa via secure video.

What if I don’t have insurance?+

If you don’t have health insurance coverage for therapy, it may be worth checking with your employer to see whether you have access to Lyra Health or another employee assistance benefit. I accept Lyra EAP when available. I also offer a prompt-pay discount for clients who are uninsured and paying for therapy themselves. Please contact me for current fee and payment information.

Do you provide couples or family therapy?+

No. Sage Therapy & Consulting currently provides individual therapy and does not offer couples or family therapy.

Do you work with children or teens?+

I do not provide therapy for children. Services for teens ages 13 and older will be coming soon. Please check back for updates about availability for adolescent clients.

What happens in the first session?+

New therapy clients first complete the secure contact form. Kay reviews your needs and availability to determine whether the practice is an appropriate fit before offering scheduling. Once you begin, your first full session is a chance to share what brought you to therapy and what you’re hoping to work on.

How do I get started?+

For therapy, complete the secure contact form first so Kay can review your needs and fit before scheduling. Therapists interested in consulting can book a free 15-minute discovery call through the client portal.
